## 💎 Brand Archetype & Story
Rooted in European gallery heritage, Atelier & Canvas pairs museum-grade paper with sustainably crafted solid oak frames. It conveys craftsmanship, bespoke artistry, and intentional home curation.

### Simplicity & Trust Factor
The word 'Atelier' immediately anchors the brand in high-end European artisanal workshops, while 'Canvas' clarifies the core product instantly. Simple, authoritative, and timeless.
