## 🛠️ 1-Minute Launch Checklist:

1. Open [ads.pinterest.com](https://ads.pinterest.com) → Click **"Create campaign"**.
2. Set Objective to **"Consideration"** and Budget to **€50 Total**.
3. Create the 5 Ad Groups above and paste the corresponding keywords and destination URLs.
4. Select the corresponding product pin from your synced catalog (or upload the framed studio images).
5. Hit **Publish**!
