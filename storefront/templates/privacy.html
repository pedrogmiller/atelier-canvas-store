Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026 • Compliant with GDPR, CCPA, and Global Privacy Standards

1. Overview

Oak Print Studio (oakprintstudio.com) values your privacy. We collect only the personal information required to process your orders, provide customer support, and improve your browsing experience. We never sell your personal data.

2. Information We Collect

  • Order Information: Name, shipping address, email address, and order details when you complete a purchase.
  • Payment Processing: Payments are processed securely via Stripe. We do not store or have access to your full credit card numbers.
  • Analytics & Cookies: We use privacy-compliant cookies and tags (including Pinterest conversion tags) to measure shopping behavior and site performance.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your shipping details are transmitted securely to our fulfillment partner (Gelato) solely for the purpose of printing, framing, and delivering your order. We do not share your information with third-party advertisers.

4. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or complete deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at hello@oakprintstudio.com.
