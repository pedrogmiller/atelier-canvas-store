Shipping & Delivery Policy

We operate a sustainable, decentralized printing network. When you place an order on Oak Print Studio, your artwork is produced and hand-framed at the professional fulfillment facility closest to your delivery address.

24–48h Production

Fine art giclée printing, custom framing, and quality inspection take 1 to 2 business days.

48–72h Delivery

Local priority courier delivery across Europe, the United States, Canada, and the United Kingdom.

Zero Customs Duties

Because items are printed locally inside your trade zone (US, EU, UK, CA, AU), you pay zero import fees.

Delivery Timeframes by Region

  • United States & Canada: 2–4 business days via FedEx / UPS
  • United Kingdom: 2–3 business days via Royal Mail / DPD
  • European Union (Portugal, Spain, France, Germany, etc.): 2–4 business days via DHL / DPD
  • Australia & New Zealand: 3–5 business days via Australia Post
  • Rest of the World: 5–8 business days via Priority Global Courier

Eco-Archival Protective Packaging

Unframed prints are wrapped in acid-free glassine paper and shipped in reinforced corrugated triangular tubes. Framed prints are cushioned with corner edge-protectors, shrink-wrapped against dust, and sealed inside puncture-resistant corrugated cartons.

Order Tracking

The moment your order leaves our local workshop, you will receive an automated dispatch email with a tracking link to follow your package to your doorstep. If you have questions, email hello@oakprintstudio.com.
